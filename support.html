Frequently asked questions

How does CarbPlanner decide when to eat?

You set a target of carbohydrates per hour and add your products (gels, bars, chews, drinks). The app spaces your feeds so intake stays even over time — larger products last longer and reserve more room around themselves. If you import a route (GPX), it also shifts feeds onto flatter ground and gets energy in before climbs, timed to how fast each product type is absorbed.

Is my data backed up?

If you're signed in to iCloud, CarbPlanner automatically saves a backup of your plans, products and history to your own private iCloud whenever they change. You can see the last backup time, and back up manually, under Profile → Data & Backup. The backup lives in your Apple account only — we can't see it. If iCloud is off, your data still works locally; it just isn't backed up until you turn iCloud on or export a file yourself.

My data disappeared / I got a new phone

Your plans and history are stored on the device, and — if you're signed in to iCloud — also backed up to your private iCloud. To move to a new phone, sign in with the same Apple ID and install CarbPlanner: when you open it on a fresh install, it offers to restore your latest backup. If nothing is offered, check that iCloud Drive is turned on for CarbPlanner (Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud) and that you're using the same Apple ID. You can also export a backup file from Profile → Data & Backup and import it on the new device.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device to change plan, cancel, or see your renewal date. In the app, use "Restore purchases" on the paywall if a purchase isn't showing up.

Is my fueling plan medical advice?

No. CarbPlanner is a planning tool, not medical or dietetic advice. Consult a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your fueling strategy, especially if you have any health condition. See our Terms of Service for details.

Product search isn't finding my item

Search results come from the public Open Food Facts database and need an internet connection. If a product isn't listed, you can add it manually with your own carb and sodium numbers.
